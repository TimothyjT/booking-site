What does Website + Online Booking do?

  1. You tell us about your business on one short call — services, hours, prices, service area.
  2. We build the website and the booking calendar. You see it and approve it before it goes live.
  3. Customers pick a time and book it themselves. You get a notice for every booking — no phone tag.

Who is Website + Online Booking for — and who is it not for?

It’s for you if…

  • Your shop has no website yet.
  • Your site is old, slow, or has no way to book.
  • You lose jobs because booking means calling during work hours.

It’s not for you if…

  • Your shop already has a modern website with booking that works.
