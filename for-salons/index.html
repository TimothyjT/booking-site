Questions salon owners ask

Can a client pick her stylist or therapist?

Yes, if that is how you work. The calendar is built around your rules — services, who offers them, and how long each one takes. If you would rather clients book the first open slot, it works that way instead.

What about walk-ins?

The website does not change walk-ins. It takes the bookings that would otherwise need a call or a message, so the desk phone rings less during the day.

Can clients move or cancel a booking?

They get a confirmation when they book. Moving or cancelling follows the policy you set — you tell us the policy on the setup call and it goes on the booking page in plain words, where clients see it before they book.

Does this take a deposit?

Not in this product. If you want a card deposit to cut no-shows, that is No-Show Guard, a separate product that can sit on a booking page like this one. This page will link to it; it will not bundle it in.
